Positional Leverage

Measures each position's gap between elite and replacement, then rewards the positions where that gap is largest. At 0 the board is pure points over replacement; at 1 the measured scarcity is applied in full. Quarterback discounts itself here — the model does not assume it, it computes it.

Production History

Players get two production modes — what they scored at full strength and what they scored without a key teammate or while hurt. Full-strength emphasis sets how far to lean toward the healthy number; games lost to an injury that has since resolved are discounted automatically on top of that.
